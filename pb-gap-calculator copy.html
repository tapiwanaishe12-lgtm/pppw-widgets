🏃 How to actually close this gap ▸
  • Easy pace should stay easy. Most of your weekly mileage (roughly 80%) should be comfortable, conversational effort — this builds the aerobic base that lets your target pace feel sustainable rather than desperate.
  • Add one pace-specific session a week. Intervals or tempo work at (or slightly faster than) your target pace teaches your body what that effort actually feels like.
  • Small gaps (under 3%) are often about pacing and race-day execution more than fitness — practice even splits in training.
  • Larger gaps (over 8%) usually need a full training block (8-16 weeks) of consistent mileage before the target is realistic.
  • Retest periodically. A recent time trial or race result is a far better predictor than an old PB — update this calculator as your fitness changes.
