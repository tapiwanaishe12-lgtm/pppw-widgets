🏅 Milestone Reward Matcher

Match the reward to the size of the achievement — ideas, not a shopping list.

Small — a good week, a small habit win
e.g. hit your planned split, logged consistently
Medium — a genuine achievement
e.g. new PB, finished a training block, a month of consistency
Big — a major milestone
e.g. finished a race, hit a long-term goal, a big life-training balance win
